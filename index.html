About Me

Hi, I’m Chang Zou. I am currently a Ph.D. student at the School of AI (SAI), Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), under the supervision of Prof. Linfeng Zhang. I graduated from Yingcai Honors College, UESTC.

My research primarily focuses on AIGC. From 2024 to 2025, my work centered on inference acceleration for diffusion models, where I achieved significant milestones in speeding up image and video generation (you may recognize my work through the TaylorSeer project). Since 2025, I have been working on step distillation for diffusion models at Tencent Hunyuan. Since 2026, my research focus has expanded to include representation learning in vision foundation models. I have also been training video VAEs at Tencent Hunyuan. Recently, I have been working on Visual Coding.

I maintain a critical yet open-minded attitude toward academic research and discussions. I am always eager to connect, feel free to reach out! By the way, I am currently seeking internship opportunities in related fields and welcome any inquiries.
